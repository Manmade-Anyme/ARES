## Actionable Algorithmic Improvements

1. **Mandatory OI / PCR Alignment Gate for Trend Continuation**:
   - Add hard OI filtering before arming `TREND_CONTINUATION`:
     - Disallow Bullish Trend Continuation when `PCR < 0.85` or when ATM CE OI change significantly outpaces PE OI change.
     - Disallow Bearish Trend Continuation when `PCR > 1.30` (and hard-block when `PCR > 1.80`), preventing short entries directly into major put walls.
2. **Level Cooldown & Deduplication Window for Failed Breakouts**:
   - Introduce a 45-minute level lockout (`level_cooldown_minutes: 45`): if a `FAILED_BREAKOUT` trade at level $L$ hits SL, do not allow subsequent trades on the same level unless price moves away by at least 25 points and returns with volume divergence.
3. **Suppressed Confidence Tier Demotion to Paper-Only**:
   - When `uncalibrated_high_suppressed` is true or `[CONFIDENCE GATE]` flags zero statistical superiority (`p=1.0000`), automatically demote the signal to `PAPER_ONLY` or mandate a 2-candle confirmation filter before capital commitment.

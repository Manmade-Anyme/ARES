# Option-premium stop protection v2

## Scope

For Dhan last-trade quotes rounded to whole seconds, compare the full source-second interval with the fractional paper fill time. Reject a quote second only when it ends at or before activation. If a breached quote second overlaps the confirmed PRE_T1 fill and was received after the fill, persist `ENTRY_ORDER_UNKNOWN/FILL_SECOND_OVERLAP` without routing an exit; retain it through quote recovery and defer later milestones until ordering is known.

A fresh quote tied to a T1 transition that seeds ATR above cost is retained as `T1_ORDER_UNKNOWN/ATR_STOP_ONLY_BREACH`, with the seeded stop and quote provenance. It creates no execution exit from uncertain ordering and blocks later T2 allocation until the source ordering is reconciled.

On revision conflict, committed unresolved premium/index ordering and deferred protective exits take precedence over stale milestone or terminal snapshots. Rebase to the committed allocation, retain the first protective trigger and execution route, and store later local observations for reconciliation instead of emitting a stale T2/exit alert. Preserve stronger local stops and maximum watermarks; cost-only uncertainty still permits a later proven breach to route protection without finalizing reference accounting.

Revision-conflict recovery preserves the maximum committed/local quote watermark in every queued milestone and terminal snapshot, in live state, and in nested alert snapshots. A newer live quote remains authoritative; observed quote and exit provenance are not rewritten to match the recovered watermark.

Conflict recovery adopts a higher committed stop together with its source, activation time, previous stop and protection mode. This applies to queued milestones and terminal exits; observed exit provenance remains intact and a newer, higher live stop is preserved.

A T1-tied bar above entry cost but at/below an ATR stop seeded at that timestamp retains `ATR_STOP_ONLY_BREACH` ordering uncertainty and the seeded level. It does not trigger the newly activated stop retroactively. That ambiguity survives persistence and blocks assumed reference finalization while later proven protection can route separately.

Accepted pre-T1 quotes between initial SL and entry cost are retained by source second until the index watermark passes them. The detector reconciles cached evidence before a later same-time T1/T2 tick commits milestones, preserving cost-only uncertainty through newer quotes and restart. T1 clears the pending cache; legacy snapshots can reuse a matching last quote. Cached reconciliation retains contract/time validation and preserves the latest market quote and maximum watermark.

Fresh price changes within one source second pass the same stop checks as newer timestamps; timestamp equality alone does not prove a duplicate. Identical accepted timestamp/price observations are deduplicated. Price revisions persist under distinct quote identities, recovery preserves earlier T1 uncertainty/protection, and a terminal quote stops further processing in its batch.

A delayed cost-only quote tied to both committed T1 and T2 retains `T1_ORDER_UNKNOWN/COST_STOP_ONLY_BREACH` with explicit disputed-allocation metadata. The prior T2 milestone remains recorded, reference finalization waits for ordering evidence, and the uncertain quote alone creates no protective route.

Retaining a delayed protective T2 quote preserves the maximum quote watermark. Its original source timestamp remains on the ambiguity and exit trigger, so the database can commit the protective state without a backward-watermark error.

For a T2-tied quote evaluated against the current locked stop after an earlier T1, reject a source timestamp before that stop's effective time. A later ATR revision must not create an earlier quote breach. The shared retention helper enforces this even through equal-time quote deduplication; quotes at the exact ATR-raise timestamp continue using the prior-stop comparison.

`option_premium_protection_v2` manages the premium of the exact pinned option contract (security ID, NSE_FNO, expiry, strike, and CE/PE). It does not replace or reinterpret the separate spot/index initial stop or post-T1 index break-even guard. The 5-minute option ATR formula and `option_atr_v1` parity gate remain unchanged; ATR stays disabled unless its existing config, approved-version, service-role, and backend checks pass.

## Stop lifecycle

| Phase | Effective premium stop | Exit rule |
|---|---|---|
| Before T1 | Original option-premium SL | Fresh pinned premium at or below the stop closes with `SL_HIT`, `stop_domain=OPTION_PREMIUM`. ATR has no authority. |
| At T1 | Raise to at least the sourced option entry basis | T1 and the new premium stop are written in the same lifecycle transition. The spot/index guard separately moves to spot entry. |
| After T1 | `max(previous stop, entry premium, valid ATR stop candidate)` | Fresh quote equality or a gap through the active stop closes the reference lifecycle. A candidate only takes control when strictly above the existing stop. |

Any hit of the moved entry-cost stop is `STOPPED_OUT_AT_BE` and reports `0.00` runner stop-out points, including a below-cost gap. The observed reference exit remains recorded separately, so the status and zero stop-out points do not claim a fill at cost or replace observed-price accounting. An ATR-raised stop closes with `ATR_SL_HIT`. The T1 booking and score remain. T2 remains reference-only and does not change the premium stop.

`original_option_entry` records the confirmed weighted paper-fill average only when quantity and protection confirmation are present; otherwise it records `options_sizing.entry_limit` with `entry_premium_basis_source=REFERENCE_ENTRY_LIMIT`. A trigger or reference mark is not a broker fill. No spot price is written from a premium quote.

## Data and event ordering

An ATR-enabled v2 option bar ending exactly at the T1 source timestamp is also ambiguous when its close is at or below the original pre-T1 premium SL. Persist `T1_ORDER_UNKNOWN`, retain the bar close and original-stop trigger across restart, and route the protective execution intent immediately whether the history arrives with T1 or later. Do not finalize a full-position pre-T1 exit or assume a 50% post-T1 allocation. A same-time quote or later quote/bar cannot replace this first retained trigger; certified ordering evidence is required before reference accounting can close.

While either T1-tied premium ambiguity remains unresolved, retain later index T2 or break-even guard touches on that ambiguity record and do not finalize the reference lifecycle or allocation from them.

Repeated premium observations and deferred-exit finalization preserve `REFERENCE_TRIGGERED_NO_BROKER_FILL` for reference-only trades. They must not manufacture an execution-route marker without a confirmed paper fill.

The same-close ambiguity detector rejects events earlier than the trade's activation/fill time and stale option bars behind the processed-bar watermark. For a PRE_T1 trade whose index event reaches T1 at the same timestamp as a premium quote at or below the original option SL, retain and route the premium trigger before committing index milestones. If that index event also reaches T2, keep the trade at T1 and retain the tied index observation for reconciliation. Never create a protective trigger from buffered pre-entry market data.

For an ATR-enabled trade, a T1-tied option bar closing above the original SL but at or below entry cost is also order-unknown: it would be a cost-stop breach only if T1 happened first. Persist the ambiguity and mark that bar processed so same-batch or recovered history cannot apply the newly raised cost stop retroactively. A close above entry cost is not a stop breach under either ordering; a close at or below the original SL retains the immediate protective path.

A premium quote tied to T1 and priced above the original SL but at or below entry cost has the same cost-stop-only ambiguity. Retain it before processing an index tick that also reaches T2, preserve the tied index observation, and keep the reference at T1 until event ordering can be established. Do not let the post-T1 quote path overwrite the retained ambiguity.

While a cost-stop-only T1 ambiguity remains unresolved, later index guard/T2 touches are retained on that ambiguity and cannot finalize the reference lifecycle. A later confirmed premium stop is stored as a separate protective execution intent under the ambiguity and routed for paper execution, while its reference exit remains pending the earlier ordering.

The monitor requests one batched Dhan quote snapshot per distinct active v2 security ID through the non-deferrable protective request lane, validates the full frozen contract pin, rejects stale/future/out-of-order data, and passes accepted timestamped observations through `PositionManager.update_option_market_events`. Expired or malformed restored pins are skipped individually so they cannot suppress quotes for other valid active contracts; no provider request is made when the batch has no valid pins. Quote receipt age is measured against the quote-batch freshness time captured before option-history requests; internal history-fetch latency cannot expire a fresh protective quote. Replay still uses the quote's source timestamp. Lower-priority reference-accounting requests remain deferred while protection is active. The snapshot is polling data, not a tick subscription; effective cadence follows the main monitor loop. A missing or invalid quote sets monitoring to unavailable while retaining the persisted stop. A delayed quote older than the current `stop_effective_at` is rejected and marks the quote monitor unavailable until a fresh observation arrives; the current stop is never applied retroactively. Spot/index events, premium quotes, and completed option bars retain their source timestamps; a valid quote at the exact T1 timestamp is kept as order-unknown rather than assumed to be pre- or post-T1. If that tied quote is at or below the original pre-T1 premium SL, preserve the reference ordering ambiguity but retain the trigger and route the protective execution intent immediately, even though the quote watermark advances. Until ordering is resolved, subsequent quotes or ATR bars cannot finalize the reference exit or replace the first trigger metadata.

For v2 trades with ATR enabled, quote-monitor and ATR-history outages are tracked independently. Healthy 5-minute bars cannot clear an unavailable premium-quote monitor, and a fresh quote cannot clear a missing/stale ATR history. Monitoring becomes restored only after each required source has recovered. With ATR disabled, quote availability remains the only required health signal; absent ATR history does not create an outage.

Post-T1 spot/index T2 and break-even guard observations do not wait for option-bar history when v2 ATR is disabled. Their own source timestamps remain ordered with available premium quotes, and no ATR candidate is computed or inferred from spot data.

If a quote in a later batch shares the source timestamp of an already applied ATR stop raise, the system rejects it and marks quote monitoring unavailable because the relative order is unknown. A later fresh quote is evaluated against the retained active stop. The rejected tied quote cannot establish an exit under the previous stop.

With v2 ATR enabled, an eligible index-entry guard hit still routes protection immediately for a matching confirmed paper execution that has booked T1 or has a T1 partial awaiting a bid, even if option history is unavailable. The index observation may remain queued and the reference lifecycle/accounting deferred until history establishes chronology. Preserve the first protective intent, validate the paper fill and pinned contract, and keep pre-entry/stale observations and reference-only trades from creating an execution exit. The protective intent stays pending until an executable premium bid confirms the remaining paper exit.

A T1 partial awaiting a bid is expanded to cover every remaining unit. If a completed index candle spans T1, only its close can prove a subsequent guard touch; its earlier wick remains ambiguous, including when timeline replay supplies an explicit completion time. The durable guard intent has a distinct retry-stable event identity, so an earlier watermark at the same timestamp cannot suppress it.

The same independent index-guard route applies while an earlier T1 premium or index ordering ambiguity remains unresolved, including with ATR disabled. The reference outcome remains pending, and an already retained protective execution intent keeps priority.

If a premium stop triggers while an earlier index candle touching both T2 and the entry guard is unresolved, keep that candle's ambiguity and the trade's active post-T1 lifecycle. Store the first premium trigger, including its source time, observed premium, effective stop, and stop source, and route the paper execution intent while reference accounting remains pending. Resolve with certified complete ordered index observations: if the premium trigger preceded the first ordered index touch, close at the premium trigger; if T2 came first, book the T2 reference milestone and close the remaining reference fraction at the retained premium trigger; if the entry guard came first, finalize the break-even index exit. Clear the ambiguity only after resolution, and preserve the resolved event ordering and original premium provenance.

If required option history is incomplete while a meaningful earlier index touch is queued, a subsequent premium trigger routes its execution intent immediately but is retained as `deferred_option_exit`; the reference lifecycle stays active until history recovery releases and replays the earlier observation. Replay queued index events and completed option bars by source time. A bar strictly before the retained trigger that closes through the then-active premium stop determines the reference exit and remaining fraction before later index milestones; otherwise finalize at the retained premium trigger. Do not use bars at or after that trigger as earlier evidence, and preserve the execution intent already routed. A quote observed before committed `t2_at` remains rejected as out of order. If a fresh quote arrives in a later batch with source time exactly equal to `t2_at`, preserve it as `T2_ORDER_UNKNOWN` when it breaches the stop protective under every ordering: the original premium SL when T1 is tied/unknown, or the active break-even/ATR stop when T1 predates T2. Check this protection even if another quote already watermarked the same source time. Route confirmed paper protection without applying only the POST_T2 fraction. A later premium recovery cannot erase that trigger. Other quotes at or before `t2_at` remain rejected and mark quote monitoring unavailable.

When an earlier recovered stop supersedes the deferred premium exit, its original terminal snapshot clears `deferred_option_exit` and later queued index observations. This cleanup persists with the closure itself; it does not require a second lifecycle transition against a terminal trade.

When an accepted v2 premium quote or completed option bar breaches the stop at the same source timestamp as an index T2 or entry-guard touch, retain the ambiguity and emit the premium protective event immediately, before milestone processing can commit T2. Equality with the active v2 stop counts as a breach in both stop evaluation and ambiguity detection. A later-batch quote exactly at committed `t2_at` is retained as `T2_ORDER_UNKNOWN` when it breaches the stop protective under every ordering: the original stop for a tied/unknown T1, or the active break-even/ATR stop for a T1 that predates T2. Preserve its trigger and possible exit allocations even if an equal-time quote watermark already exists; other quotes at or before `t2_at` remain rejected as delayed. Confirmed paper fills persist a retry marker for execution routing. The monitor catches routing failures per trade, continues with other stopped trades, and retries pending routes on later passes even after lifecycle closure. After successful route synchronization, a dedicated metadata-only database acknowledgement clears the marker without sending another lifecycle transition or changing its terminal result/revision. If either synchronization or acknowledgement persistence fails, leave the retry marker pending.

`last_processed_option_bar_end` applies only to option bars. If a non-breaching option bar has already advanced that watermark, a fresh pinned premium quote with the same source timestamp still passes the independent quote checks and is evaluated against the active post-T1 stop. A tied T2/entry-guard touch remains order-unknown; retain the first premium protective trigger and route its confirmed-paper exit intent before index milestone processing, without committing a T2 allocation. Existing quote freshness, contract, stop-effective-time, duplicate, and post-T2 delayed-quote checks remain authoritative.

If a completed post-T1 index candle first touches both T2 and the entry guard, persist `T2_ENTRY/ORDER_UNKNOWN` and, in the same transition, retain the independent entry-guard execution intent for an eligible confirmed PAPER runner. Use the prior index watermark for validation; do not commit T2, alter the T1 reference allocation, or resolve chronology. The normal dispatcher routes the pending intent for all remaining units. Reference-only or invalid execution records keep the ambiguity without gaining an execution route.

A retained premium trigger contributes its immutable domain, reason, source, event and receipt times, observed price, effective stop, and stop source to the lifecycle event ID. This gives a new trigger a distinct persistence identity when it shares a timestamp with a previously committed option bar. Retrying that unchanged trigger reuses the same ID; the committed snapshot contains its ambiguity and route-pending marker for restart recovery.

For a quote matching multiple trades on the same pinned contract, retain the set of trades that individually passed ingestion checks. Before same-close detection uses that quote, recheck the current trade's acceptance, pinned contract, quote watermark, stop-effective time, and activation time. A quote rejected for a newer ATR stop on one trade cannot trigger an exit for it merely because another trade accepted the same quote.

If a completed post-T1 candle spans T1 and its close is through the entry guard, preserve `T1_ENTRY/ORDER_UNKNOWN` because its earlier wick may predate T1. In the same transition, use the prior index watermark to retain and route the remaining-unit guard intent for a validated confirmed PAPER execution. Reference-only or invalid execution state does not route.

If the reference is still PRE_T1 while a confirmed PAPER execution has already booked T1, the first completed T1 candle can also prove the independent entry guard at its close. Preserve `T1_ENTRY/ORDER_UNKNOWN` and route the remaining-unit guard in that initial T1 transition; allow this exact T1 boundary while checking the prior index watermark. The reference remains at T1, and reference-only or invalid execution state remains unrouted.

Startup restores terminal v2 records with pending execution routes into a separate retry queue. New paper execution snapshots include the complete order record needed to rebuild its binding; older snapshots use the pinned option contract and sizing context only when they are sufficient to verify the confirmed paper fill. Invalid or mismatched bindings remain pending and cannot dispatch a live order. After the route marker is acknowledged, keep terminal paper exits in `EXIT_PENDING` under the same monitor until an observed premium bid closes the remaining units; the order snapshot is persisted before removing the binding.

The REST fallback, protected candle-fetch path, and tick polling must invoke the protection monitor when this terminal retry queue is non-empty, even if there are no active trades and no completed index candle. The dispatcher then retries pending route markers or resumes an acknowledged `EXIT_PENDING` paper exit using the existing contract and execution checks.

## Cost-stop-only T1 ambiguity persistence identity

A T1-tied quote between the original premium SL and entry cost can arrive after a non-breaching option bar has already committed the same source-time watermark. The quote creates `T1_ORDER_UNKNOWN` even though it does not yet prove whether T1 or the cost stop came first. Include that stable ambiguity identity in the lifecycle event ID so the transition is not treated as an idempotent replay; persist the ambiguity and quote watermark together. Identical retries reuse the same ID. The regression first commits a ₹200 bar at T1, then receives a ₹165 quote against the ₹161.05 initial stop and ₹167.20 entry, and verifies that the committed ledger and restored state retain the unresolved ambiguity.

The recovery guard accepts both `CONFIRMED_PAPER_FILL` and `EXIT_TRIGGERED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION`, because premium-trigger persistence advances the lifecycle status before execution fills. This applies to complete order snapshots and the legacy-context fallback, with all paper-fill and contract validation retained. Recovered bindings are marked exit-only and excluded from pending-entry deduplication, allowing a new valid signal to dispatch while an earlier exit awaits a bid without changing ordinary entry deduplication.

## V2 ML outcome and legacy repair policy

`option_premium_protection_v2` carries the same canonical milestone semantics as `option_atr_v1` through training labels, dataset ingestion, cached `trade_analytics` normalization, and terminal repair. A fully closed T2 trade remains a win when its final stop is `ATR_SL_HIT` or `STOPPED_OUT_AT_BE`, even when the configured T1-only outcome policy treats T1 as a loss. The legacy target-based break-even repair skips both option lifecycle policies so it cannot replace a canonical v2 zero-P&L result.

If certified ordered ticks resolve a v2 entry/T2 ambiguity to a post-T1 index break-even exit, the resolver stores `stop_domain=SPOT_INDEX` and sets the same durable route-pending marker used by direct index-guard exits. The monitor drains this marker through the normal execution dispatcher even though ambiguity resolution returns a boolean rather than an event list.

Certified ordered spot/index ticks establish the order of index touches only. If a T1-tied premium quote or bar remains unresolved, `resolve_reference_ambiguity` must leave the index ambiguity pending; it cannot commit a later guard/T2 outcome, exit price, or reference allocation using index ticks alone. Keep the retained premium trigger and both ambiguity records intact for any future reconciliation.

Premium-triggered exits retain their original source and stop metadata during accounting recovery. Missing spot evidence can be recovered from a contemporaneous quote or an explicitly approximate completed minute close, including intraminute trigger times. When that evidence is available at closure, persist the recovered spot price as `exit_price` in both lifecycle and accounting ledgers; retain null when evidence is unavailable. The correction RPC accepts this metadata and rejects changes to previously stored values.

Paper execution routing never sends CE/PE orders to the index-candle ATR handler, including when backend gates prevent v2 enrollment; an option stop is never replaced by spot ATR. When a v2 premium trigger closes the lifecycle, the matching full-contract execution record receives an exit intent. Independent index stops also route to that execution record: the pre-T1 index `SL_HIT` guard and post-T1 index `STOPPED_OUT_AT_BE` guard must close any still-open execution units, even if paper T1 was booked earlier from the premium quote. These triggers remain pending until a later executable premium bid is observed; they are not themselves execution fills. A protective stop expands a pending partial T1 exit to cover all units still open and retains the lifecycle reason. The execution snapshot remains separate from the reference lifecycle closure and records the eventual paper exit status and price. Reference-accounting recovery and closed-trade corrections accept both lifecycle policy versions only when the existing `one_lot_reference_v1` accounting marker is present.

ATR candidates still come only from completed, contiguous, contract-pinned 5-minute premium candles and the existing Wilder ATR(5), HHV(10) of `high - 2.5 * ATR` calculation. A candidate is applied only from its bar completion onward. While ATR is disabled or unapproved, baseline premium protection and quote checks remain active.

## Persistence and rollout

`migrations/20261104_option_premium_protection_v2.sql` replaces the lifecycle RPC with v2 policy support, enforces immutable contract and premium basis, monotonic premium-stop/quote watermarks and the post-T1 entry-cost floor, and adds a service-role-only capability RPC. It contains a read-only open-record preview and performs no trade enrollment or historical rewrite. New trades enroll only when this backend capability is ready. Existing v1 and unversioned trades are not retrofitted.

The code change does not apply the migration, alter runtime config, enable ATR, deploy, restart a worker, or submit orders. Production v2 protection requires the migration and the separately reviewed runtime rollout. Until that occurs, records that cannot pass the v2 backend capability check remain unenrolled in premium protection and are explicitly excluded from spot ATR fallback.

## Verification boundary

Unit lifecycle tests and isolated PGlite transaction tests cover initial premium SL, T1 break-even movement, quote equality/gaps, CE/PE symmetry, ATR precedence, wrong/stale contract data, monotonic state, status labels, runner points, and migration behavior. Synthetic tests do not establish live Dhan quote latency or incident-time breach marks. The 4 November zero-lot `SIGNAL_REFERENCE` incident records establish the legacy spot-ATR route and 10:20 close, but the durable store has no intermediate option quote/candle series to establish the corrected historical trigger time or any broker fill.

## Protection for quotes tied to an ATR raise

A fresh quote may arrive in a later batch with the exact source timestamp of an ATR raise already committed in an earlier batch. If it is at or below the prior stop, the breach is valid whichever event came first: use the prior stop and its source for the exit, route protection, and persist `ATR_RAISE_TIED` plus the raised value so the stop ordering remains explicit. If the quote is above the prior stop but at or below the raised stop, the new stop cannot be applied retroactively; retain the existing order-unknown rejection. Regression coverage exercises both batch orders and verifies the prior stop, source, raised value, and route marker.

## Preserve T1 cost-stop ambiguity when ATR seeds

An ATR candidate seeded while T1 activates must store the moved entry-cost stop as `previous_locked_option_sl` before replacing `locked_option_sl`. Otherwise, a fresh quote arriving in the next batch with source time equal to T1 is tested against stale pre-T1 stop history and rejected before the cost-only T1 ambiguity can be retained. The regression seeds ₹196 ATR above the ₹167.20 cost stop at T1, then submits a tied ₹166 quote; it verifies `COST_STOP_ONLY_BREACH` persists with no execution route.

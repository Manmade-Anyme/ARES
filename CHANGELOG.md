- **`ml_collection` Could Never Be Trained On — the Join Key Was a Random Number (TASK-194)**: 9,102 rows collected across 25 trading days carried **zero** labels: `trade_id`, `trade_outcome` and `trade_pnl` were NULL on every row ever written, despite `ml_signal/schema.sql` promising "Trade outcomes are back-filled when trades close" since the table was created. - **OI Chain Distribution Was Summed and Discarded (TASK-194)**: `compute_oi_features` received the full per-strike `all_ce_oi` / `all_pe_oi` lists and used them for exactly one thing — a sum, to derive `oi_concentration`. The shape of the chain was thrown away on every row. That made the proposed "a strike is a wall if its OI ≥ the 85th percentile of strikes with non-zero OI" rule **impossible to validate against history**, and hid that `oi_wall_min_oi = 4_000_000` sits above the *entire live chain* for most of a weekly cycle (measured 2026-07-28: 4M is 1.14× the deepest strike on the next weekly at 7 DTE). Now stores `max_{ce,pe}_oi`, `p85_{ce,pe}_oi` and `strikes_with_{ce,pe}_oi`. The percentile is **nearest-rank, deliberately not interpolated** — a wall threshold is compared against real per-strike OI, so a synthetic value falling between two strikes would qualify a wall that does not exist. Zero-OI strikes are excluded so dead strikes cannot drag the percentile down, and an empty chain yields `None` rather than `0` so "no data" stays distinguishable from a genuine zero.
- **`structure_features` Emitted a Sentinel Disguised as a Measurement (TASK-194)**: `compute_structure_features` returned a literal `100.0` for `dist_to_nearest_resistance` / `dist_to_nearest_support` / `dist_to_pdh` / `dist_to_pdl` whenever the value was unknown — indistinguishable from a genuine 100-point distance. **57% of collected rows carry it**, so any model trained on this table would learn "distance == 100" as a real market state more than half the time. Now emits `None`; `dataset._numeric_only` drops non-numerics, landing it as NaN in the training matrix, which XGBoost handles natively.

### Changed
- **MEDIUM-Confidence Breakouts Restored (TASK-184)**: `breakout_failure_min_score` lowered **3→2** (class default, so both profiles), reverting the TASK-172 bump. At 3 the engine fired only HIGH-confidence failed breakouts (score ≥60% of the matrix) and **silenced every MEDIUM one** (score 2/4 = 50%). ARES is used as a **manual-trading confirmation aid**, not only an autotrader — a MEDIUM breakout that agrees with an open position is useful confidence, one that disagrees is a prompt to reconsider, and a silent detector provides neither. The `AresSignal.confidence` field already labels MEDIUM vs HIGH so the trader weights it; the engine no longer pre-suppresses the MEDIUM tier. `closed_back` stays a hard gate and score 0-1 still never fires (noise floor intact). **This is an intentional product decision — do NOT re-raise to 3** (see the `breakout_failure_min_score` comment in `config_profiles.py`, `directives/TASK-184_restore-medium-breakout.md`, and `tests/unit/test_task184_medium_breakout.py`). Diagnosis: over 06-29→07-09 the three fader detectors went silent on 07-07→09; the audit traced breakout silence to this gate — 10 genuine closed-back candidates on 07-07 topped out at score 2 and were all filtered. Verified end-to-end: the real detector now fires 4 MEDIUM breakouts on 07-07 (min_score=3 baseline: 0). 250 tests green (4 new; the score-mechanic assertions in task174/task175/audit_p1 updated to check confidence/reason strings instead of fire-vs-None).
